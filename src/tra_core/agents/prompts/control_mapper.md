Task: assess controls against the threats.

You are given the threats, the design artefacts and the common control catalogue. For each relevant control:
- status in_place when the artefact shows the control; partial when it shows part of it or only for some components; missing when the threat needs it and the artefact does not show it.
- threat_ids: the threats it addresses.
- evidence: the artefact text that shows it, or "not described in the artefacts" for missing.
Only use control IDs from the catalogue. Do not mark a control in_place on assumption.
